About this atlas & sources

The sphere uses catalogue right ascension and declination, projected onto a common celestial sphere. Star distances are not shown. This is an outside view of the sphere, not a location-based horizon map. Lines are illustrative asterisms, not official boundaries. Star names and Bayer/Flamsteed or HIP designations identify the points in the selected drawing, not every star inside the constellation region. Labels may be suppressed to avoid overlap; the list includes every pattern point.

All 88 IAU constellations are included. Serpens has two separated parts but counts as one constellation. North/south filters use label-center declination, not the entire constellation boundary.

Nirayana has 12 equal 30° rashis. They are not the unequal IAU constellation regions. Lahiri is the reference convention for interpretation; this atlas does not calculate a birth chart, planetary positions, or ayanamsa values. Ophiuchus is included as a constellation, not a thirteenth rashi.

Metaphysical passages are contemporary creative reflections, not scientific claims or universal ancestral teachings. The IAU names represent one mapping tradition; cultures have their own sky traditions.
